Team

The Safety Stack is built and maintained by a small team mapping what AI safety techniques exist, which ones frontier labs appear to use, and where the gaps are. All of our data, taxonomies, and code are public and updated every week.

Jacob Livingston Slosser

Sapien Institute · Arb Research

Jacob leads the taxonomy of safety techniques, defining how interventions, monitors, and evaluations fit into layers and how published work gets mapped onto them. He also designed the site’s visual story and the stack by lab view.

Tomáš Gavenčiak

Arb Research

Tomáš started The Safety Stack and builds the weekly pipeline that collects, screens, and annotates papers, forum posts, and system cards. He also maintains the catalogues of models and organisations and built the model-by-technique matrix.

Finn Hambly

Arb Research

Finn works on the probabilistic inference layer, combining public evidence and forecasting to track which safeguards frontier labs are deploying. He focuses on modelling safeguard overlap and correlated failures to monitor live risk-surface coverage.

Dan Elton

Metascience Observatory

Dan co-built the early versions of The Safety Stack, helping put together the first data pipeline, early lab-stack reconstructions, and open-source evidence collection. He now advises the team.

Gavin Leech

Arb Research · Paradigm 3

Gavin advises the team on research direction, annotation and inference methods, and how to make the data and tools useful to researchers and funders.

Shared infrastructure

The Safety Stack builds on the Shallow Review of Technical AI Safety and shares data and pipeline code with its 2026 edition.
